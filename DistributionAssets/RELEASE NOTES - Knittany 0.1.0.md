# Knittany 0.1.0

The first release of Knittany, a Mac app for regrading knitting patterns to the
person you're knitting for, and for designing sweaters from scratch.

## What it does

- **Regrade patterns** to anyone's measurements: between or beyond the published
  sizes, with targeted changes like a longer body, and to your own gauge. Ease
  comes from the designer, or is worked out from the pattern's sizes.
- **People** with their body measurements, and estimates from the Craft Yarn
  Council's women's or men's size chart.
- **Import pattern PDFs**, read on your Mac or with Claude, then check and correct
  what was read before saving.
- **Design a sweater** from scratch: a top-down raglan or round yoke, pullover or
  cardigan, with your choice of neck, fit, length, shape, sleeves and edges.
- **Knit mode**: the regraded steps as a checklist with a row counter.
- **PDF export** with a schematic drawn to the regraded measurements, and the
  designer's chart pages.

## Good to know

- This build isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet; see FIRST OPEN.
- Universal: runs on Apple silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 15 Sequoia or later.
