KNITTANY — OPENING IT FOR THE FIRST TIME

1. Drag Knittany into your Applications folder.
2. Open Knittany.

This build isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so the first time you
open it macOS says it can't check the app for malicious software. To open it
anyway:

  - Click Done (not Move to Trash).
  - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message about
    Knittany, and click Open Anyway. Confirm with your password or Touch ID.

You only need to do this once. Later releases will be signed and notarized,
and this step goes away.

Your library (people, patterns and projects) is kept in
~/Library/Application Support/Knittany and is backed up by Time Machine.
